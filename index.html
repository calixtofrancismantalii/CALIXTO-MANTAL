01. About Me

Back then, I started as a working student and spent several years as a graphic designer. I created numerous designs—including logos, posters, and social media content—and even collaborated with well-known political personalities, where my work was used for campaign and project advertisements.


With years of experience in graphics, I naturally transitioned into web design, using my visual skills to build interfaces and bring them to life through code.


After completing my college degree in Information Technology, I shifted my career to web development and landed roles as a front-end developer, building multiple websites for B2B companies. Over time, I expanded my technical expertise and now have experience as a full-stack developer, allowing me to work confidently on both the front end and back end of web applications.


A fun fact about me: I love reading books and listening to rap music. I’m also a big sneaker enthusiast—I enjoy finding unique pairs that reflect my style and energy.
